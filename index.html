Good questions to ask

You don't have to figure it all out alone. These starters help.

Your manager

  • "What are your top priorities for me in the first 30 days?"
  • "How do you prefer to communicate — async or sync?"
  • "What does success look like in this role after 90 days?"
  • "Any common pitfalls I should watch out for?"

HR team

  • "What benefits am I eligible for, and when do they begin?"
  • "How do I submit expenses or reimbursements?"
  • "Where can I find the employee handbook?"
  • "Who do I contact for payroll questions?"

Your buddy

  • "What's the unwritten rule everyone here knows?"
  • "What tools do you wish you'd known about sooner?"
  • "Who are the go-to people when you're stuck?"
  • "What's the best way to make a good first impression?"
